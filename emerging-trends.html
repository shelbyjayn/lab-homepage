Emerging Trends in Defense Technology

Autonomy is the theme connecting most of what is new in defense right now. Drones that once required a dedicated pilot can now fly, navigate, and even coordinate with other drones with minimal human input. Software is increasingly treated as the core product, with hardware built around it rather than the other way around.

Electronic warfare and counter-drone systems have also become a priority as cheap commercial drones show up on battlefields worldwide. At the same time, the Pentagon is investing heavily in simulation and training tools, including virtual reality, to prepare soldiers faster and at lower cost.

Software defined warfare means the advantage no longer belongs to whoever has the biggest fleet, but to whoever can update their systems the fastest.

A few trends worth watching:

  • Autonomous drone swarms
  • AI-assisted electronic warfare
  • VR and simulation-based training
