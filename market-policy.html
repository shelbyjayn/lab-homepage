Market & Policy Shifts in Defense

The Pentagon has traditionally relied on a small group of prime contractors like Lockheed Martin and Raytheon for major weapons systems. That is starting to change. New procurement pathways, such as the Defense Innovation Unit and Replicator initiative, are letting the Department of Defense buy directly from smaller, faster-moving tech companies.

Venture capital has taken notice. Billions of dollars have flowed into defense-focused funds over the past few years, a sharp reversal from a decade ago when many investors avoided the sector on ethical grounds.

The character of war is changing, and the country that adapts its industrial base the fastest will hold the advantage.

Some key terms worth knowing:

  • Prime contractor
  • Replicator Initiative
  • Dual-use technology
